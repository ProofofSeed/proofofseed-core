72 B (64 char)                51390 B        51462 B         72 B

The full statement at 2048 rounds is 52454598 B per response, so a 12-character
passphrase made secret costs 20 B more per response: 0.0000% .

⚠️ The salt's *length* stays public either way — it is the number of input words —
so this hides the passphrase's value, not its length nor the fact that one exists.
